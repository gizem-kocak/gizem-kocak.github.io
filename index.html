Gizem Koçak is an interior architect graduating from Mimar Sinan Fine Arts University in Istanbul, with a GPA of 3.33/4.00. Her studio work moves comfortably from concept and space planning through to technical drawing and detailing, with a particular interest in sustainable design, material honesty, furniture design, and visual merchandising across residential, commercial, and institutional projects.

Practical experience spans both a design-office internship — concept development, technical drawing, and material board preparation — and a construction-site internship following interior implementation work in the field against the original drawings.
